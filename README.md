# Sales and Return Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyses sales performance and product returns across different months, product categories, countries, customers, shipping durations, and markets.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Sales:** Measures overall sales performance.
- **Total Returns:** Tracks the volume of product returns.
- **Total Orders:** Shows the overall order volume.
- **Return Rate:** Measures the proportion of orders or sales returned, according to the dashboard's defined calculation.

## Key Insights
1. **Sales and Profit Trends:** The monthly area chart compares total sales and total profit over time, helping identify changes in business performance and periods that may require further investigation.

2. **Returns by Product Category:** The bar chart compares total returns across product categories, helping identify categories that contribute more heavily to returns.

3. **Geographical Return Analysis:** The country-level map shows how returns are distributed geographically, supporting comparisons between markets.

4. **Customer-Level Return Analysis:** The customer chart highlights differences in total returns across customers, helping identify customers or accounts that may warrant further review.

5. **Shipping Duration and Returns:** The shipping-days chart explores the relationship between shipping duration and returns, helping investigate whether delivery time is associated with return patterns.

6. **Market and Category Comparison:** The market-level chart compares return rates across markets and product categories, supporting the identification of areas with potentially higher return risk.

7. **Interactive Year Filtering:** The year slicer allows users to filter the dashboard by year and examine how sales and return patterns change over time.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate product categories with high return volumes or return rates.
- Review customer and geographical return patterns to identify possible operational issues.
- Examine shipping duration alongside return trends before drawing conclusions about delivery-related causes.
- Monitor sales, profit, order volume, and return rate together to assess overall business performance.

## Conclusion
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales performance and return behaviour. It supports product, customer, geographical, and operational analysis to help identify areas for further investigation and improve data-driven decision-making.
